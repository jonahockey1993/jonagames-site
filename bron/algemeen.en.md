# Privacy in our games

JonaGames makes games for Android. Each game has its own privacy policy, because not every game does the same things. Below are the principles that apply to all our games, followed by the full policy for each game.

## What applies to all our games

- You don't need an account and we have no server of our own. **We do not receive or store any personal data about you.**
- Your progress is stored only on your own device.
- You only see an advert if you choose to. Adverts come from Google AdMob; in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the game asks for your consent first.
- Purchases go entirely through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
- Our games are not directed at children under 13.
