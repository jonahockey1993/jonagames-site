# Privacy Policy — Lily Logic

> **Draft, written 18 September 2026.** Describes the app as it will be at launch (with ads, purchases and Play Games). Not legal advice: review it, fill in the brackets, and keep it in sync with the *Data safety* form in Play Console.

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

Lily Logic is a puzzle game for Android by JonaGames ("I", "we"). Questions? Email info@gereed3d.nl.

## In short
- You need no account and we run no server. **We do not receive or store any personal data about you.**
- Your progress lives only on your own device.
- You only see ads when you choose to (in return for a reward). They are served by Google AdMob, and Google processes data to do so.
- Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.

## What the app stores on your device
Progress and stars, a level in progress, settings, your firefly balance and purchased skins. This data does not leave your device through us. If Android backups are enabled, Android may include it in the backup of your own Google account. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything.

## When the app uses the internet
**Date of the daily puzzle.** To stop the daily puzzle from being manipulated by changing the clock, the app asks Android for network time or makes a short request to a Google server. As with any internet request, your IP address is visible to Google. No other information is sent.

**Ads (Google AdMob).** If you choose to watch an ad, the app loads it through Google AdMob. Google collects, among other things: your IP address (from which a general location may be derived), your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your interactions with the ad, and diagnostic information. Google uses this to serve ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first; without consent you only get non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under *Settings → Privacy options* in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.
More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Google Play Games (optional).** If you sign in, Google processes your Play Games player ID, your leaderboard scores and your achievements. Other players may see your name and score, depending on your own Play Games settings. You manage or delete this data in the Play Games app.

**Purchases.** Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details, only a confirmation that a purchase was made.

## Legal basis
For running the game: performance of the contract and our legitimate interest (a fair daily puzzle). For personalized ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Transfers outside the EU
Google may process data outside the European Union, relying among other things on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, you exercise these rights for ads and Play Games with Google (https://myaccount.google.com). I am happy to answer questions at info@gereed3d.nl. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

## Changes
When the app changes, we update this policy. The date at the top shows when that last happened.
